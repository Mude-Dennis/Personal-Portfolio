01 About

I don't just want to understand how technology works. I want to build things with it.

I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student, and most of my time outside lecture halls goes into taking a problem apart, building a working version of the solution, and figuring out whether it can stand on its own as something real.

My work moves between hardware and software: microcontrollers, sensors and circuits on one side, and the code, data and interfaces that make them useful on the other. I've applied this mostly to agriculture and energy so far, because they're areas where a small technical improvement can have an outsized practical impact.

Alongside the engineering, I care about the business case for what I build — whether an idea can grow past a working prototype into something people would actually use.
